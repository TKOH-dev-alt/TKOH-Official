🌟 Who We Are

TKHO

We believe mental healthcare should be accessible, compassionate, and effective for every person — wherever they are in their journey toward inner peace and emotional balance.

Our Philosophy

Mental health is not a destination but a continuous journey. We hold space for every stage — the breakthroughs and the struggles alike. Our platform is built on the belief that seeking help is not weakness, but profound courage. Every session, every conversation, every breath is a step toward your truest self.

Our Mission

To democratise access to quality mental health support by connecting people with compassionate, trained professionals and intelligent digital tools. We build bridges between vulnerability and healing, between isolation and connection — so that no one faces their inner battles alone.

Our standard

We aim to make mental wellness easier to approach: clear information, gentle self-guided practices, and honest boundaries about what digital tools can and cannot do. TKHO is not an emergency service or a replacement for qualified clinical care.

⚖️ Legal

Trust, Confidentiality & Policy

Understand how we protect your data, your rights, and your safety on our platform.

Data Collection & Privacy

TKHO is still configuring its production account, consent, and data-governance systems. Until those systems are formally documented, use the public wellness tools without entering sensitive clinical information.

End-to-End Encryption

Do not treat this early-stage website as a secure clinical record or emergency channel. Security and privacy controls will be documented before sensitive care workflows are enabled.

Data Retention

Clinical session data will only be collected after the relevant account, consent, retention, and support systems are fully configured. Until then, do not submit sensitive clinical information through this early-stage website.

Scope of Services

TKHO currently provides general wellness tools and a directory prototype. Any professional relationship, credentials, availability, and scope of care must be confirmed before using a listed service.

Therapist Obligations

Practitioner verification and clinical governance workflows are not yet represented as complete on this public prototype. Do not rely on this page alone to assess a provider.

User Responsibilities

Users agree to engage in good faith, provide accurate health information relevant to their care, and respect the professional boundaries of their therapist. Misuse or abusive behaviour toward practitioners may result in immediate account suspension.

Crisis Response Protocol

TKHO does not currently provide emergency response or guaranteed clinical follow-up. If you may harm yourself or someone else, contact local emergency services or a qualified crisis service immediately.

Mandatory Reporting

Therapists are required by law to report credible threats of harm to self or others, and indicators of child abuse or neglect. TKHO provides clear protocols and fully supports practitioners in fulfilling these obligations while maintaining therapeutic alliance wherever possible.
