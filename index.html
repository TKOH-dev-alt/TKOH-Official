Who we are

TKHO

We believe mental healthcare should be accessible, compassionate, and effective for every person — wherever they are in their journey toward inner peace and emotional balance.

Our Philosophy

Mental health is not a destination but a continuous journey. We hold space for every stage — the breakthroughs and the struggles alike. Our platform is built on the belief that seeking help is not weakness, but profound courage. Every session, every conversation, every breath is a step toward your truest self.

Our Mission

To democratise access to quality mental health support by connecting people with compassionate, trained professionals and intelligent digital tools. We build bridges between vulnerability and healing, between isolation and connection — so that no one faces their inner battles alone.

Our standard

We aim to make mental wellness easier to approach: clear information, gentle self-guided practices, and honest boundaries about what digital tools can and cannot do. TKHO is not an emergency service or a replacement for qualified clinical care.

Trust, Confidentiality & Policy

Understand how we protect your data, your rights, and your safety on our platform.

Confidentiality commitment

TKHO treats your conversations and booking details as private. We collect only what is needed to provide the service, use access controls around account information, and do not sell personal conversations or therapy details.

Thoughtful data choices

You can choose what to share with the AI Buddy. For therapy, your psychologist will explain what they record, why they record it, and how you can ask questions about your care.

Informed consent for therapy

Before a session, your psychologist will discuss the nature and goals of therapy, confidentiality and its limits, fees, cancellation terms, and how to raise concerns. You remain in control of whether you continue, pause, or change providers.

Verified professionals

TKHO reviews listed psychologists' identity and professional credentials before publishing profiles. You should still ask any provider questions about fit, approach, and scope of practice before beginning care.

Clear AI disclaimer

The AI Buddy offers emotional support and reflection. It is not a psychologist, cannot diagnose medical conditions, and is not a substitute for therapy, medication, or emergency care.

If you need urgent help

If you may hurt yourself or someone else, contact local emergency services or one of the helplines above now. The AI Buddy cannot monitor emergencies or guarantee a response.
