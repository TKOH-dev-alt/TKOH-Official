🌟 Who We Are

TKOH Wellness

We believe mental healthcare should be accessible, compassionate, and effective for every person — wherever they are in their journey toward inner peace and emotional balance.

Our Philosophy

Mental health is not a destination but a continuous journey. We hold space for every stage — the breakthroughs and the struggles alike. Our platform is built on the belief that seeking help is not weakness, but profound courage. Every session, every conversation, every breath is a step toward your truest self.

Our Mission

To democratise access to quality mental health support by connecting people with compassionate, trained professionals and intelligent digital tools. We build bridges between vulnerability and healing, between isolation and connection — so that no one faces their inner battles alone.

⚖️ Legal

Trust, Confidentiality & Policy

Understand how we protect your data, your rights, and your safety on our platform.

Data Collection & Privacy

We collect only the minimum data required to provide mental healthcare services. Personal identifiers, session content, and clinical notes are never shared with third parties without explicit consent. We comply with India's DPDP Act 2023 and international GDPR standards.

End-to-End Encryption

All video sessions use AES-256 encryption with ephemeral session keys. Your conversations, mood logs, and health data are encrypted at rest and in transit using industry-leading protocols. Not even TKOH staff can access your session content.

Data Retention

Clinical session data is retained for the legally mandated minimum period. You may request complete deletion of your account and all associated data at any time via your account settings or by contacting our Data Protection Officer at privacy@tkohwellness.com.

Scope of Services

TKOH Wellness connects users with supervised psychiatry students under licensed clinical supervision. Sessions are supportive and therapeutic in nature and do not replace emergency psychiatric care or crisis intervention services from licensed hospitals.

Therapist Obligations

All practitioners hold valid credentials, maintain professional liability insurance, and undergo regular clinical supervision. They are bound by applicable professional ethics codes, confidentiality obligations, and TKOH's clinical governance framework.

User Responsibilities

Users agree to engage in good faith, provide accurate health information relevant to their care, and respect the professional boundaries of their therapist. Misuse or abusive behaviour toward practitioners may result in immediate account suspension.

Crisis Response Protocol

When our AI chatbot or a therapist detects indicators of acute crisis, the platform immediately presents emergency resources and — with the user's consent — alerts our on-call clinical supervisor who will follow up within 15 minutes via phone.

Mandatory Reporting

Therapists are required by law to report credible threats of harm to self or others, and indicators of child abuse or neglect. TKOH provides clear protocols and fully supports practitioners in fulfilling these obligations while maintaining therapeutic alliance wherever possible.
